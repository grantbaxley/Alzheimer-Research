# Validation (out of sample)

Quantities never used in fitting, compared with published long-term data. The two drug comparisons are open-label extensions against external natural-history controls (evidence grade C: no randomized control after 18 months, about half of participants dropped out); the progression check comes from pooled observational cohorts, with dementia approximated as CDR-SB ≥ 4.5. Version 5 failed that progression check (92–98% vs 70%), so version 6 added person-to-person variation in tau propagation and resilience and was refit with it as a target; it is shown here for completeness but is no longer an independent test.
