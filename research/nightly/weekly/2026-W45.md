## The week in one paragraph

This was a week of **discipline over enthusiasm**: two of the catalogue's most prominent ideas were
**parked** after failing their own falsification tests, a borrowed "firebreak" analogy was **killed**
in silico, and a flagship repurposing idea's claimed novelty was **superseded** by a 2026 preprint.
The recurring wall held firm across independent nights — **the irreversible floor is neuron loss, so
every route (vascular, synaptic, metabolic) caps at "freeze," and the useful window is early.** But
the week also produced three genuinely forward leads, all pointing the same way — toward **timing and
mechanism-matched repurposing** rather than chasing a biomarker number: (1) the field appears to have
trialed the **wrong MAO isoform** for the earliest tau seed; (2) a cerebrovascular **tipping point** is
the one place the "alternative stable states" story stops being metaphor; and (3) a **sex-aware
amyloid-chronicity clock** whose decisive test uses only open data and has not yet been run. A
repo-integrity bug and a newly-found paper retraction were also fixed.

## Most promising ideas, ranked (with evidence grade and next test)

1. **maoa-dopegal-wrong-isoform-repurposing** *(preclinical)* — the LC's earliest-tau mechanism is
   MAO-A → DOPEGAL → AEP → tau-N368 (Kang/Ye, *JCI* 2020, 10.1172/JCI130513), yet the only completed
   MAO-inhibitor AD biomarker RCT used MAO-**B** (rasagiline). A safe, cheap, approved MAO-**A**
   inhibitor aimed at DOPEGAL has never been trialed. **Next:** a ≥12-week biomarker target-engagement
   study — does it lower CSF DOPEGAL / tau-N368 in amyloid+ MCI? (decisive negative if not).
2. **sex-amyloid-tau-window** *(observational, investigating)* — re-anchored this week to **amyloid
   chronicity** (time-since-amyloid+), not age, because the robust human sex effect sits on the
   amyloid→tau *coupling* step, the same node as the leverage point (Coughlan 2025, PMID 40029638,
   **verified**). **Next:** the open ADNI/BioFINDER/WRAP analysis — does chronicity (± LC-MRI) predict
   tau rate beyond age, and does a sex term add? The ingredients are all positive; the integrated paper
   does not yet exist.
3. **vascular-bistability-hysteresis** *(in-silico, investigating)* — a published cerebrovascular model
   derives a real saddle-node + hysteresis from the amyloid→pericyte-constriction→hypoperfusion loop
   (Ahern/Goriely, *Proc R Soc A* 2025, 10.1098/rspa.2024.0548), first arm empirically real in human
   tissue (Nortley/Attwell 2019, 10.1126/science.aav9518). **Next (empirical, not modeling):** is a
   vascular readout bimodal at matched amyloid, and does post-clearance CBF show path-dependence?
4. **drug-target-mr-pipeline** *(observational)* — stand up open-data drug-target Mendelian
   randomization + colocalization, the project's one route to a *causal* estimate without restricted
   data. **Next:** validate on known controls (statins null; ACE direction) before trusting it.
5. **improvement-endpoint-validation** *(observational, investigating)* — re-scoped: measure
   **normalization of the practice-learning curve**, not raw change (the practice effect is both the
   threat and an AD signal). **Next:** can a passive/aggregated at-home measure detect the within-person
   rebound after one night of recovery sleep, above its retest band?

## Ideas parked or killed (the honest column)

- **PARKED — treat-to-target-proximal-biomarker** (was the catalogue's #1): p-tau217 is a validated
  *readout*, not a validated *target*. No trial-level surrogacy exists, and the drug that most cleanly
  lowered tau still missed cognition with the best signal at the **lowest** dose (diranersen/CELIA —
  the repo's own graded claim, grade C, **re-checked**). Survivable kernel spun out as a biomarker
  *futility-gate*, never a graduation surrogate.
- **PARKED — regime-shift-trial-signatures** (trial-signature half): its own falsification test fails —
  published anti-amyloid person-level distributions are unimodal and overlapping with no second mode
  (Espay/Kepp/Herrup, *eNeuro* 2024, 10.1523/ENEURO.0319-23.2024), and the dose-response was graded.
- **KILLED (in silico) — the topological "firebreak/islanding" resilience idea**: down-weighting
  network bottlenecks does not contain an autocatalytic tau front; only full disconnection does (which
  a brain cannot do). Reframed as **resilience-is-growth-not-topology**: resilience lives in the growth
  term, not the wiring.

## Ideas weakened or downgraded (not killed)

- **lithium-microdose-target-trial**: its claimed novelty ("the design, not the drug") was **superseded**
  by a 2026 active-comparator target-trial-emulation preprint (modest, heterogeneous RR ~0.84–0.87,
  Optum null, not peer-reviewed, about progression not prevention); reframed toward the resilience-buffer
  *mechanism*, kept at preclinical, not promoted.
- **vaccine-trained-immunity**: the mechanism half **strengthened** (recombinant out-protects live
  vaccine, favouring trained immunity), but the headline female-predominance was recorded as
  **mechanism-agnostic**, not as support — a correction to the earlier write-up.
- **The X-linked MAO-A sex sub-hypothesis** was **demoted** to a tagged mechanism-to-test: human MAO-A
  shows dosage compensation (no clean double-dose), and the estrogen link is contradictory (MAO-A peaks
  in perimenopause, not with estrogen loss).

## New evidence that moved the picture (with sources)

- **Faster female tau is at the coupling step, not amyloid load** (Coughlan 2025, PMID 40029638,
  JAMA Neurol 82(4):364–375, **verified**): in Aβ+ preclinical adults, women accumulate tau faster
  (inferior temporal, fusiform, lateral occipital); sex does not modify amyloid rate.
- **The earliest tau seed has a druggable node** (Kang/Ye, *JCI* 2020, 10.1172/JCI130513): MAO-A →
  DOPEGAL → AEP → tau-N368 in the locus coeruleus (mouse/cell).
- **Deferiprone (iron chelation) failed with harm** (Ayton, *JAMA Neurol* 2025,
  10.1001/jamaneurol.2024.3733): accelerated decline — a cautionary "remove the toxic metal" result.
- **A newly-found retraction** added to the integrity watchlist: Montagne et al. 2018 *Nat Med*
  ("Pericyte degeneration causes white matter dysfunction," 10.1038/nm.4482), retracted April 2024.
- **Repo-integrity bug fixed**: the microdose-lithium catalogue entry had silently merged into the
  vaccine entry since 2026-10-23 (a missing YAML id line); restored, so both now load their own data.

## Modeling results (with honest limits)

Six toy models this week; each is a labeled illustration, not calibrated to the disease.
- **Cascade-leverage** (11-02): *which* link in amyloid→tau→neurodegeneration you block barely matters;
  *when* you start dominates — banked irreversible damage sets the ceiling.
- **Silent-engram window** (11-04): recoverable benefit is front-loaded and capped by the surviving-
  neuron ceiling — the "treat early" wall from a new mechanism (alive-but-silenced vs dead).
- **Firebreak/percolation** (11-05): the topological-resilience analogy fails (see killed, above).
- **Improvement-detectability** (11-06): naive endpoint-minus-baseline reads a false +0.25 SD
  "improvement" under a true null (pure practice); a practice-curve-deviation estimator recovers it,
  but only with dense at-home sampling and high reliability.
- **Vascular-hysteresis** (11-07): a bistable perfusion collapse whose reversal needs over-correcting
  the driver ~3× below the collapse point — but only if feedback is strong enough; the model cannot say
  whether a real brain is in that regime.
- **LC "why there"** (11-08): the LC's structural load makes it the seed origin (undruggable), while
  the MAO-A/DOPEGAL branch is the one druggable accelerant (~60% onset delay when blocked).

## Access & collaboration opportunities

*The project owner is a non-scientist with no wet lab. Each item: a source and why it matters. Honest
about what needs nothing vs. an application, fee, or institution.*

**Zero barrier — open now, no login, no application:**
- **AMP-AD Agora** (agora.adknowledgeportal.org) and **Open Targets** (platform.opentargets.org, disease
  EFO_0000249). *Why:* open the gene pages for **MAOA, LGMN (AEP), DBH, ADRA2A** (the LC-seeding axis)
  and **PDGFRB, RGS5, KCNJ8, ABCC9, ANPEP** (pericytes) and note each "nominated target? / genetic score."
  This confirms or kills our "mechanistically hot but genetically cold" reading — and both research
  assistants hit a wall because the pages are JS-rendered. A human can do it in ~10 minutes.
- **Alzforum Therapeutics database** (alzforum.org/therapeutics, free). *Why:* it tabulates every tau /
  anti-amyloid drug's biomarker-vs-cognition result — the raw material to test the biomarker-futility-gate
  idea without any data access.
- **M2C2 / NIH Mobile Toolbox** (open-source apps). *Why:* generate your own repeated high-frequency
  at-home cognitive data at zero cost — the substrate for the practice-curve-deviation endpoint.
- **MarkVCID / MarkVCID2** (markvcid.partners.org). *Why:* an open NIH vascular-cognitive-impairment
  biomarker consortium (WMH, PSMD, cerebrovascular reactivity); directly serves the vascular ideas and
  is worth a registry record. (Published validation framework is readable with no application.)

**Low barrier — free registration or a light-touch request (no institution, no fee):**
- **AD Workbench** (alzheimersdata.org) — free account; the lowest-barrier route to some repeated-measures
  data (e.g. GAP BioHermes). *Why:* the one realistic path to a repeated at-home dataset. **A free
  Synapse account** similarly unlocks open-access summary datasets.

**Genuinely gated — needs an institution, a DUA, or is off-limits per our rules:**
- **Trial individual-patient data** (CLARITY-AD, TRAILBLAZER-ALZ2) and **ROSMAP / AD Knowledge Portal
  participant-level data** are DUA-gated; our data policy forbids processing participant-level DUA cohort
  data, so these stay off-limits — we use open summary statistics and `alzres.synthetic` instead. The
  only no-access move for the vascular bimodality / lithium matched-pathology questions is digitising
  already-public figures.

**Nothing requires a fee, an IRB, or a wet lab this week** — the decisive next steps are open-data
analyses plus the handful of human point-and-click lookups above.
