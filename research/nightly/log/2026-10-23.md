# Night of 2026-10-23: Safe drugs with a reason to work

Repurposing lens under a hard cost ceiling (< $100 per person per year), revisiting
`fn1-clearance-restore`. Two parallel research assistants ran: a cheap-candidate scan with an
RCT graveyard, and a vaccines/trained-immunity scan
(`scratchpad/nightly-2026-10-23/repurposing.md`, `vaccines.md`). I read both in full and
verified the night's pivotal fact (VALAD) directly against Europe PMC.

> Research hypotheses, not medical advice; no one should self-dose anything here. Claims carry a
> DOI/PMID; the assistants read mostly abstracts and flagged preprints. Integrity watchlist: both
> scans screened their sources — nothing rests on Lesné (Aβ*56), Masliah, or Cassava/simufilam.

## Questions asked

- Which cheap, approved drugs have both a mechanism and a *human* signal (natural experiment,
  claims data, Mendelian randomisation)?
- Which of those already failed an RCT, and what did the failure show?
- What active-comparator or target-trial-emulation design would make an observational signal
  credible? (this question became tonight's spine)
- Revisit: did the FN1 papers test lowering fibronectin *after* pathology (restoration), and is
  there a cheap drug that does it?

## What I found (with sources)

**The RCT graveyard is large and recent — repurposing enthusiasm keeps dying in trials.**
Valacyclovir (VALAD, Devanand, JAMA 2026, PMID 41405855) was not just negative but *worse* than
placebo (ADAS-Cog between-group +3.93, p=.01, verified) — a direct hit to the HSV-antiviral
hypothesis. Oral semaglutide (EVOKE/EVOKE+, PMID 41865758) was negative and *both trials were
discontinued*. Valproate caused *harm* (accelerated atrophy, PMID 21917762). NSAIDs (ADAPT),
minocycline (MADE, PMID 31738372) and doxycycline+rifampin (DARAD) all failed. The recurring
shape: a promising observational signal, then a null or harmful RCT.

**The cost gate itself killed the most-hyped candidate.** GLP-1 agonists fail < $100/year outright
(no generic), and they *also* failed EVOKE — so the drug everyone is excited about is excluded on
both grounds. Valacyclovir is dose-dependent: it passes the gate at a low suppressive dose but
*fails* at the 4 g/day dose that actually failed in VALAD.

**The surviving positive signals are observational or small/old, and shrink under scrutiny.**
Lithium has the freshest mechanism (2025 Nature, PMID 40770094: endogenous brain lithium is
depleted in AD via amyloid sequestration; replacement rescues mice via GSK3β) but the rescue is
preclinical and the human RCTs are tiny (Forlenza, n=45). Metformin's protective claims signal is
near-null in Mendelian randomisation for AD specifically (PMID 35902387). Sildenafil's striking
claims signal (PMID 35572351) attenuates under active-comparator designs. B-vitamins help only in
the high-homocysteine subgroup (VITACOG).

**The one class with a strong quasi-experimental signal is vaccines — and the signal looks
antigen-independent.** A regression-discontinuity natural experiment on the Welsh shingles rollout
found ~20% lower dementia (PMID 40175543; replicated in Australia and Canada). Crucially, the
*recombinant* shingles vaccine protects at least as well as the *live* one (different antigen;
PMID 39053634), an adjuvanted RSV vaccine shows a similar effect, and unrelated vaccines (BCG,
Tdap, influenza, pneumococcal) each associate with lower risk — a pattern that fits *trained innate
immunity* better than suppression of any one virus. Big caveat: cohort hazard ratios (0.49–0.74)
are much larger than the de-biased RD estimate (~0.80), and an active Tdap comparator attenuates
one from 0.49 to 0.73 — healthy-vaccinee bias is real and large, and no RCT with a clinical
endpoint exists.

## New ideas

Three that survive, plus one created-and-killed (detailed in `ideas.yaml`):

1. **vaccine-trained-immunity** (understand) — the night's strongest. The vaccine–dementia signal
   is antigen-independent (trained immunity), with a clean falsification: if antigenically
   unrelated vaccines (BCG, Tdap) don't protect while only herpesvirus-directed ones do, the
   hypothesis is wrong.
2. **lithium-microdose-target-trial** (slow) — microdose lithium, made credible not by another
   tiny trial but by an active-comparator target-trial emulation (lithium vs other mood
   stabilisers) that defeats healthy-user bias.
3. **repurposing-credibility-gate** (understand) — adopt a fixed active-comparator / regression-
   discontinuity bar before the repo grades any observational repurposing claim above the weakest
   level. Its value equals the size of the corrections it forces (shingles 0.49→0.73; sildenafil
   attenuation; metformin's null MR).
4. **antiviral-hsv-reactivation** — created and immediately **killed**: VALAD settled it (negative,
   trending to harm).

## Revisited: fn1-clearance-restore

Advanced new → investigating. Both FN1 papers reconfirmed; the 2026 Nat Aging abstract says
reducing fibronectin "rescues" BBB function (suggestive of restoration) but does not say whether
FN1 was lowered *after* established pathology or co-suppressed from baseline — the full text is
paywalled, so the restoration-vs-prevention crux is still open (now the idea's `next`). No cheap,
approved, brain-penetrant FN1-lowering drug exists (pirfenidone isn't CNS-targeted and likely
fails cost; FUD/pUR4 is experimental; losartan/metformin lower *peripheral* fibronectin but no
brain evidence). Genetically anchored, but neither clearly reversal-capable nor cheaply druggable.

## Modeling

`scripts/power_2026_10_23.py` — tonight's lens doesn't map onto the tau-spreading tools, so this
is trial-design arithmetic (numpy/scipy), not a disease model. (1) The bias gap: cohort HRs of
0.49/0.67/0.74 vs the RD estimate ~0.80 imply the cohorts overstate the effect by 1.08–1.63×. (2)
Events needed in an active-comparator design (80% power): HR 0.80 needs ~631 dementia events,
HR 0.85 ~1,189, HR 0.90 ~2,829. Reading: a credible design is powered only for the larger
de-biased effects; if the true effect is as small as HR 0.90, you need thousands of events, which
is why the attenuated signals are so hard to confirm honestly. Design arithmetic only.

## Dead ends and surprises

- **The research stopped me proposing a dead idea.** I had planned an antiviral/HSV repurposing
  idea; the VALAD result (negative *and* worse than placebo, published this year) killed it before
  I wrote it. Logged as a killed entry rather than quietly dropped.
- **Surprise:** the most decisive evidence for the vaccine signal argues *against* the intuitive
  mechanism — it's probably not about herpesviruses at all, but general immune training.
- **Pattern worth a rule:** every observational repurposing signal tonight shrank under a stronger
  design. That recurrence is itself the case for idea 3.
- **Honest limit:** not one candidate tonight has a positive confirmatory AD RCT.
