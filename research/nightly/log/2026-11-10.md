## The night's through-line: a big observational signal that vanishes under causal restriction

Both independent axes told the **same story** — a large observational association that does not
survive Mendelian randomization or RCT:

- **Network hyperexcitability (interneuron):** observational late-onset-epilepsy → dementia HR ~3.05
  (ARIC, PMC7836657), but MR runs mainly **AD → epilepsy** with the reverse near-null (OR ~1.05;
  Fang 2023, PMID 37225432), and three low-dose levetiracetam trials were **null on cognition**
  (HOPE4MCI CDR-SB diff −0.10 ns, NCT03486938; LEV-AD overall null, PMID 34570177 — only the
  epileptiform-positive subgroup improved). So hyperexcitability is best read as a **downstream
  accelerant you can measure but have not shown you can treat for prevention**; the causal preventable
  fraction is ~0.
- **Myelin / white-matter hyperintensity (oligodendrocyte):** the most-cited "WMH is causal" paper
  (JAMA Netw Open 2024, PMID 38776079) reports WMH → AD-liability MR OR ~1.43 **but its own
  genetic-WMH score → ALL-CAUSE dementia is null (HR 1.02, p=0.07)**, with a paradoxical protective
  BP-MR signal (a pleiotropy flag) — the causal headline rests on an AD-GWAS-liability outcome with
  by-proxy cases. SPRINT-MIND slowed WMH but missed its dementia primary (PMID 30688979).

In both, AD GWAS heritability sits in **microglia, not neurons or oligodendrocytes** (Nott 2019;
Bryois 2020) — so both "start from this cell type" framings swim against the genetics, and the
defensible mechanism runs *through* microglial clearance.

## Revisited: remyelination-prevention-window → INVESTIGATING (prevention rationale downgraded)

Kept alive (the AD remyelination-trial gap is genuine — **no** remyelination agent has been trialed
in MCI/preclinical AD with a myelin-imaging endpoint; gap confirmed), but **re-graded evidence from
RCT → observational** (the "RCT" was clemastine's MS conduction-latency *surrogate*, not AD
prevention) and the prevention premise explicitly downgraded, because the myelin/WMH→dementia causal
chain fails both MR (null, above) and RCT (SPRINT). The preferred non-anticholinergic agent,
metformin, has a dementia signal only from target-trial emulation and drug-target MR (~14% lower AD
odds via mitochondrial complex I; 10.1038/s41467-022-35157-w, 10.1007/s00125-022-05743-0), with the
first RCT (MAP, NCT04098666) unread until ~2027. **Sharpest next test** (zero new data): does
within-person myelin change (MWF/R1) predict processing-speed slope *after adjusting for WMH + amyloid*?
If myelin's partial R² over WMH+amyloid ≈ 0, a dedicated myelin endpoint is redundant.

## New ideas (fresh cell type: interneuron; plus the metformin pivot)

1. **epileptiform-gated-lev-prevention** (slow, none) — a low-dose levetiracetam *prevention* trial
   gated on amyloid+ AND sleep-EEG epileptiform+ (the enrichment the null trials lacked). Kill: no
   PACC/CDR-SB slowing even in the enriched subgroup.
2. **sleep-eeg-hyperexcitability-screen** (detect, observational) — at-home overnight sleep-EEG as a
   cheap population screen (~90% of discharges are in sleep). Kill: it adds no incident-decline
   prediction beyond plasma p-tau217.
3. **metformin-benefit-concentrates-in-high-wmh** (slow, observational) — test whether metformin's
   dementia signal concentrates in the high-WMH subgroup (a treatment×WMH interaction). Kill: no
   interaction → the effect isn't myelin/vascular-mediated.

## Modeling

`scripts/preventable_fraction_2026_11_10.py` — a preventable-fraction toy (PAF via Levin's formula;
onset delay via Gompertz incidence, β=ln2/5.5). Two honest results. (1) The **PAF collapses** under
causal restriction: a generic factor with observational RR 2.0 at 30% prevalence gives PAF ~23%, but
a causal RR of 1.2 gives ~6%; the two real axes (epilepsy RR 3.05→MR ~1.05; WMH all-cause-dementia
MR null) drop to ~0. (2) Because dementia incidence rises so steeply with age, a **5-year onset delay
requires roughly HALVING risk** (HR ~0.53) — a modest causal RR of 1.2 buys only ~1.5 years even if
eliminated. Sober implication: prevention bets must be sized by *causal* effect, and a marker that
fails MR and RCT (as WMH and hyperexcitability so far do for dementia) is not yet a lever. Structural
illustration; literature RRs but assumed prevalences; not calibrated epidemiology.
