## What I found (with sources)

**The causal root is not the highest-yield target.** Common-variant genetics puts the dominant
*modifiable* signal in microglial/immune genes (TREM2, PLCG2, INPP5D, MS4A, ABI3, SPI1, BIN1;
Bellenguez 2022, PMID 35379992); amyloid overproduction (APP/PSEN) is the causal root only in
autosomal-dominant AD; APOE is the single largest effect everywhere. The weakest link
*interventionally* is **tau→neurodegeneration** — tau-PET predicts atrophy best, yet every anti-tau
antibody with confirmed target engagement has missed its clinical primary (bepranemab/TOGETHER
slowed tau-PET 33–58% but missed CDR-SB). The most leverageable near-term node is the
**neurodegeneration→symptoms arrow** (resilience/reserve repeatedly decouples pathology from
symptoms). So clearing amyloid gives real-but-capped benefit because it hits the root, not the
leverage point.

**Approved-drug MR, honestly:** the cleanest coloc-backed signal at an approved-drug target is **ACE
— and its direction argues *against* ACE inhibitors** (genetically lower ACE → *higher* AD risk,
independent of blood pressure; PMID via Alz Res Ther 2025). GLP-1R MR looks protective but the
EVOKE/EVOKE+ trials were null (a wrong-time-vs-wrong-target disagreement). IL-6R is contested;
statins/PCSK9 are null-to-adverse; TREM2 agonism has the strongest genetics but no approved drug
(AL002 failed Phase 2). Net: few approved drugs have a clean *causal* case, and the strongest one is
a *harm* flag.

**Breadcrumbs from other projects / AI agents (standing goal b):** Open Targets' top genetic AD
targets (PSEN1, CR1, APP, TREM2, APOE, PLCG2, MS4A6A, ACE, CD33, ABCA7, BIN1) are essentially
*undrugged* (known-drug score ≈ 0). AI research agents — Biomni-AD and Prima Mente (2026 Alzheimer's
Insights AI Prize winners), TxGNN, FutureHouse Robin, Google's AI co-scientist — have **mostly
confirmed/duplicated this same microglial+lipid target list rather than extended it with
gate-passing approved-drug pairs** (Robin's lead is dry-AMD, not AD; the co-scientist demos aren't
AD). Honest read: the breadcrumbs so far *converge on what we already have*, which is itself
information — the frontier target set is agreed and under-drugged.

## New ideas

Three (detailed in `ideas.yaml`):

1. **target-the-leverage-node-not-the-root** (slow) — search approved drugs by the modifiable genetic
   node (microglia/immune, lipid/APOE), not the amyloid root. Kill: best microglial target isn't
   drugged; node matters less than timing (tonight's model).
2. **ace-inhibitor-ad-pharmacovigilance** (understand) — a *harm* hypothesis: test whether ACE
   inhibitors raise AD risk (MR direction), via an ACEi-vs-ARB active-comparator claims analysis.
   Novel because it's a harm signal, not a benefit hunt. Explicitly not medical advice.
3. **drug-target-mr-pipeline** (understand) — stand up the open-data drug-target MR + colocalization
   capability; the project's first route to an actual *causal* estimate, on summary data only, with
   APOE/proxy controls and known-control validation first.

## Revisited: repurposing-credibility-gate

Advanced to investigating with a concrete **five-point gate** (active comparator; negative controls;
regression discontinuity where a cutoff exists; drug-target cis-MR + colocalization direction-matched;
APOE exclusion + non-proxy GWAS primary). Worked examples: shingles promotes (survives + has an RD
natural experiment), sildenafil and metformin stay weakest, and tonight's ACE case shows criterion 4
catching a false "protective" story. It's a **proposal for policy/VETTING.md pending owner sign-off**
— I did not edit policy.

## Modeling

`scripts/cascade_leverage_2026_11_02.py` — a 4-node cascade (amyloid→tau→neurodegeneration→cognition)
blocking each link in turn. Two honest results: (1) *which* link you block barely matters (T→N, closest
to the damage, is marginally best, ~0.03 over clearing amyloid; all beat no-treatment); (2) *when* you
start dominates — the same block gives cognition ~0.66–0.69 early vs ~0.26–0.29 late, because banked
irreversible damage sets the ceiling. First version saturated (all zeros) and I retuned it; recording
that. Consistent with the recurring start-early finding. Linear toy, assumed rates, not a drug claim.
