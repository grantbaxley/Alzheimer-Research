## What I found (with sources)

**The most interesting finding: remyelination's dose-response is expected to be an inverted-U, which
breaks standard trial design.** Opicinumab (anti-LINGO-1) is the case — in SYNERGY (Cadavid, *Lancet
Neurol* 2019, PMID 31285147) the best signal was at an *intermediate* dose with benefit falling off
at the top dose, so the pre-specified **linear dose-trend test was null (p≈0.89)** and the trial
missed; the follow-up (AFFINITY) then bet on a **single fixed dose** — exactly the wrong move for a
bell-shaped curve. This is mechanistically expected (an optimal OPC-differentiation window; over-drive
is harmful), so it is a **class property** to design around, not a fluke.

**Every remyelination trial has stopped at the imaging surrogate.** Clemastine (ReBUILD, PMID
29029896) moved VEP latency, not disability; opicinumab never cleared a clinical primary; bexarotene
(BEAT-AD, PMID 26822146) failed cognition. The AD-side parallel: BP control slowed WMH but not
dementia (SPRINT-MIND, PMID 30688979 / 31408137); anti-amyloid antibodies drive amyloid-PET to
near-zero yet only slow decline. A myelin imaging readout is necessary, not sufficient — never an
approvable endpoint.

## Revisited: oligodendrocyte-amyloid-source-test → INVESTIGATING (largely resolved, leans GO)

The feared "does remyelination *raise* amyloid?" penalty looks **backwards** on current evidence. The
victim-vs-culprit "contradiction" largely **dissolves**: the two headline papers are **same-lab
(Nave)** and complementary, not opposing — myelin dysfunction drives amyloid (Depp 2023, *Nature*,
PMID 37258678 — loss-of-function only; they did *not* remyelinate-then-measure), while oligodendrocytes
*do* make Aβ but **cannot seed plaques without a neuronal threshold Aβ**, so the oligo source is
subordinate (Sasmita 2024, *Nat Neurosci*, PMID 39103558; ~50% of human gray-matter oligodendrocytes
carry APP/BACE1 mRNA in AD — real machinery, but expression ≠ plaque contribution). **Independent
gain-of-function evidence** outside the three cited papers: the remyelinating drug **clemastine lowers
Aβ** and rescues memory in APP/PS1 mice (Xie 2021, PMID 34746130). So the balance leans **lower (GO)**,
not closed (all causal data are mouse; clemastine has muscarinic off-targets; overexpression model).
**Two corrections recorded:** (1) the cited Blanchard 2022 (PMID 36385529) has *no* amyloid readout —
it is human APOE4-myelin context, not an amyloid-source result; (2) AD GWAS heritability enriches in
**microglia, not oligodendrocytes** (Nott 2019; Bryois 2020), so the mechanism likely runs
oligo-myelin ↔ microglial clearance (BIN1, an Agora-nominated "Myelination" target), not
oligo-amyloid-source in isolation.

## New ideas

1. **remyelination-nonmonotone-trial-design** (understand, in-silico) — design for the inverted-U:
   ≥4 doses, a curvature-aware primary, a responder-mixture endpoint. Modeled tonight. Kill: if a
   multi-dose analysis never beats two-arm/monotone power across the plausible region.
2. **remyelination-amyloid-factorial-gonogo** (understand, preclinical) — a 2×2 factorial
   (remyelination × anti-amyloid) in a humanized knock-in with an oligo-Bace1 arm to settle
   raise-vs-lower + interaction. Kill: the combined arm clears less amyloid than anti-amyloid alone
   (penalty → NO-GO).
3. **surrogate-gated-myelin-trial** (slow, observational) — use myelin imaging as a go/no-go *gate*,
   not an endpoint. Kill: in a cohort, the myelin surrogate change does not predict processing-speed
   change (gate uninformative).

## Modeling

`scripts/nonmonotone_dose_trial_2026_11_09.py` — a trial-design toy (synthetic, SD units) with
SYNERGY-like doses {0, 3, 10, 30, 100}. Honest results: for an **inverted-U** drug (interior peak),
the two-arm top-dose test (power **0.03**) and the monotone linear-trend test (**0.03**) are
near-powerless — the top dose is on the downslope and the trend averages the up/down slopes to ~0,
exactly SYNERGY's p≈0.89 — while an **omnibus ANOVA (0.41)** and an orthogonal **quadratic curvature
contrast (0.59)** recover the effect. An honest **counter-case** keeps the method from looking
magic: for a **truly monotone** drug, the two-arm (0.50) and linear-trend (0.56) tests win and the
quadratic loses (0.20) — the curvature machinery pays *only* when the biology is genuinely
non-monotone, which for remyelination it is expected to be. Synthetic; not a drug claim.
