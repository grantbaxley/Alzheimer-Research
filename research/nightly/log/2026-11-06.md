## Red-team: treat-to-target-proximal-biomarker → PARKED

The repo's highest-ranked idea (borrow HIV/oncology treat-to-target, aimed at tau/p-tau217 rather
than amyloid) does **not survive** its own falsification test. The distinctive claim — that steering
a tau *number* steers cognition — is contradicted by the best available data:

- **p-tau217 is a validated READOUT, not a TARGET.** FDA clearances are diagnostic only; there is no
  published trial-level surrogacy analysis for p-tau217, and amyloid's "hollow surrogacy" is the
  cautionary precedent (Ackley 2021, 10.1136/bmj.n156 — amyloid reduction does not substantially
  improve cognition).
- **A near-direct falsifier:** the drug that most cleanly lowered tau still missed cognition, with
  the *best* signal at the *lowest* dose (diranersen/CELIA — see the repo's own `evidence/claims.yaml`
  `diranersen`), and N-terminal/aggregated-tau antibodies engaged tau with no cognitive benefit
  (gosuranemab, semorinemab/LAURIET, tilavonemab, zagotenemab). This is hollow surrogacy recurring
  one marker inward.
- **The winners don't rescue it:** lecanemab (10.1056/NEJMoa2212948) and donanemab
  (10.1001/jama.2023.13239) lowered p-tau217 *and* slowed decline, but they are anti-amyloid drugs
  (confounding tau-lowering with upstream amyloid removal), and neither shows the p-tau217 change
  *size* predicts the cognition change *size*.

**Verdict:** downgraded `new → parked`. The survivable kernel is spun out as
`biomarker-futility-gate-platform` — use p-tau217/MTBR-tau for enrichment and as a go/no-go *drop*
rule, never to graduate an arm. The decisive prospective test (does maximal tau lowering help?) is
DIAN-TU Tau NexGen (NCT05269394), not yet read out.

## Revisited: improvement-endpoint-validation → INVESTIGATING (re-scoped)

Survives, but only in a narrow form. **Active cognitive tasks cannot cleanly show improvement:**
retest/practice effects mimic ~0.2 SD gains (FINGER's both arms rose, 10.1016/S0140-6736(15)60461-5;
Jacobs 2017, 10.1016/j.trci.2017.08.010), and the only domain with a proven *at-home* within-person
recovery is vigilance (PVT after recovery sleep, 10.3758/s13428-016-0763-8), not AD-relevant episodic
memory. **The key reframe:** the practice effect is *also* an AD signal — its attenuation tracks
amyloid and entorhinal tau (Tort-Merino 2025, 10.1002/alz.087332) — so the ruler should measure
*normalization of the practice-learning curve*, not raw change (spun out as
`practice-curve-deviation-endpoint`). On reliability, only high-ICC burst-aggregated measures (M2C2,
ICC 0.89–0.97, 10.1002/dad2.12283) clear the bar; passive measures that escape practice by
construction (speech acoustics, ICC>0.65, 10.1186/s13195-024-01543-3) have the weakest reliability so
far — that tension is the crux. **Also fixed an error in the idea card:** its practice-effects source
was the PACC *decline* composite (10.1001/jamaneurol.2014.803), not a practice-effects paper; swapped
for FINGER + Jacobs. Sharpest next test: does a passive/aggregated at-home measure register the
within-person rebound after one night of recovery sleep, above its own retest band? Days, not months.

## Modeling

`scripts/improvement_detectability_2026_11_06.py` — a detectability toy (dimensionless, SD units).
Two honest results. (1) The smallest within-person change detectable is
`1.96·√2·√((1−ICC)/ICC)/√k`; only ICC≈0.9 **and** dense burst sampling (k large) push it below the
0.2 SD practice-mimic floor (ICC 0.6 never does here). (2) With a saturating practice curve present,
naive endpoint-minus-baseline reads a **false +0.25 SD "improvement" under a true null** (it is pure
practice), while fitting the practice curve to pre-onset samples and taking the post-onset residual
recovers ~0 under null and ~+0.29 under a real 0.3 SD gain — but only with enough dense early samples
to fit the curve, and only if its shape is modelled right. Dense *at-home* sampling is the lever on
both counts. Structural toy; not calibrated to any measure.
