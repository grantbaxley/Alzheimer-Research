## What I found (with sources)

**The three night-1 ideas did not survive intact.**

- **`detection-vs-biological-resilience` → killed.** Its cornerstone datum — equal hippocampal
  atrophy but better female verbal memory (Sundermann 2016, PMID 27029635) — is at least as
  consistent with *genuine* reserve as with a test artifact, and the group that found it reads it
  as reserve (PMID 39951912). The "under-protected at the synapse" half is contradicted by the
  largest CSF synaptic study (Oh 2025 *Nat Med*, n=3,397, PMID 40164724): the synapse→cognition
  axis shows **no sex interaction**. Both halves fail. The surviving kernel — women decline
  *faster per unit tau once symptomatic* (PMID 37641484, PMID 41540505) — is cognitive
  vulnerability, not synaptic under-protection, and is kept as an observation, not this idea.
- **`synaptic-reversal-endpoint` → parked (wrong as written).** The panel is directionally
  incoherent for an "up = good" readout: neurogranin, YWHAG and VAMP2 *rise* with damage while
  NPTX2, NRN1, VGF *fall*, and YWHAG:NPTX2 rises with both decline and normal aging (PMID
  39380095, PMID 40164724). In the only interventional CSF data, anti-amyloid therapy moved
  damage markers *down* (DIAN-TU gantenerumab, PMID 38683602; sabirnetug, PMID 39955261); no
  human trial has pushed the protective markers *up* (only a preclinical mouse Nptx2 boost). A
  marker-specific reframing survives; the simple upswing endpoint does not.
- **`sex-amyloid-tau-window` → investigating (one objection fell, one sharpened).** The
  off-target tau-PET worry is largely *defeated*: an off-target-immune fluid tau marker tracks
  tau-PET with no sex interaction (MTBR-tau243, Robinson 2026, PMID 42191949), so "faster female
  tau" stands. But the real kill is that the amyloid-to-tau interval is confounded with age at
  amyloid onset and amyloid burden and adds little after tau positivity (PMID 42309020); it is
  better described as an amyloid-level × age × sex threshold (PMID 41485137). "Time by interval,
  not age" largely collapses into "amyloid burden + age," which existing enrichment approximates.

**On the vascular/BBB biology itself, the honest answer to "cause or consequence" is: probably
both.** The strongest pro-causal human data is prospective — baseline CSF sPDGFRβ (a
pericyte-injury marker) predicts later cognitive decline in APOE4 carriers after adjusting for
Aβ/tau (Montagne 2020 *Nature*, PMID 32376954; Nation 2019 *Nat Med*, PMID 30643288) — but it is
observational, largely single-group (Zlokovic/USC), and "independent of Aβ/tau" is a covariate
adjustment, not proof. An independent group shows the *reverse* arrow (Aβ oligomers constrict
capillaries via pericytes; Nortley 2019 *Science*, PMID 31221773), so the relationship is
plausibly a bidirectional feed-forward loop. A 2026 mouse meta-analysis calls the APOE4–BBB
literature "inconsistent" (PMID 42098772). Skeptic's counterweight on vascular risk: SPRINT MIND
(PMID 30688979) *missed* its primary probable-dementia endpoint (stopped early, underpowered) and
was significant only for MCI/composite, and Mendelian randomisation finds blood pressure/lipids
*not* causal for AD specifically (Larsson 2017, PMID 29212772) — consistent with vascular risk
acting through vascular/mixed dementia more than "pure" AD. The clearest vascular-resilience
genetic signal is FN1 rs140926439 (OR 0.29 in APOE4 homozygotes, replicated; PMID 38598053, PMID
41195593, PMID 42728404). And the dominant neurovascular event in the clinic today is
iatrogenic: ARIA from anti-amyloid antibodies, driven by cerebral amyloid angiopathy (PMID
40063015, PMID 31827267). One caution the scan flagged: do **not** state "sleep clears amyloid via
the glymphatic system" as settled — convective bulk flow and AQP4-dependence are genuinely
contested (Nedergaard vs Verkman vs Hladky & Barrand; PMID 41289996).

## New ideas

Three, all vascular/BBB, detailed in `ideas.yaml`:

1. **clearance-threshold-onset** (understand) — model onset as a *clearance-capacity tipping
   point*, not a seeding event, and test for **hysteresis** (a point past which restoring
   clearance no longer reverses pathology, which would explain why late anti-amyloid works
   poorly). Seeded by tonight's model. Kill risk: the threshold is near-tautological in the toy
   model, and autosomal-dominant disease shows seeding matters on its own.
2. **vascular-reserve-aria-dosing** (slow) — turn the neurovascular unit from a liability into a
   dosing variable: does a pre-treatment vascular-integrity readout (microbleed/CAA burden +
   sPDGFRβ) predict ARIA beyond APOE4 + microbleed count, enough to personalise anti-amyloid
   dose? Falsifiable on banked trial data with no new trial.
3. **fn1-clearance-restore** (slow) — lower excess BBB fibronectin to phenocopy the protective
   FN1 loss-of-function variant in APOE4 carriers, tested as *restoration* after pathology, not
   just prevention. Kill risk: fibronectin is systemically essential; benefit may be
   developmental.

## Revisited: detection-vs-biological-resilience

Killed (see above). The falsification test proposed on night 1 — do women show equal/lower
synaptic markers at matched tau? — is answered "no sex interaction" by Oh 2025, so the idea's
prediction fails. A genuine gap remains: no study reports sex-stratified
synaptic-marker-vs-tau slopes directly, so the *exact* test is still worth running, but the
hypothesis as stated is dead.

## Modeling

`scripts/clearance_2026_10_21.py` — extends the repo's FKPP spreading model with a linear
clearance term `-γ·x` (the model has no clearance term, so it cannot express the vascular idea
without one). Holding a single seed fixed, mean final burden falls from 0.40 (γ=0.6) to ~0 (γ≥1.0),
with a sharp threshold between γ=0.8 and 0.9 — i.e. the *same* seed clears or spreads depending
only on clearance. **Honest caveat:** this is close to tautological — the low-burden growth rate
is ~(α−γ), so a threshold near γ=α (=1.0) is algebra, not biology. What it buys is a concrete,
testable framing for idea 1 and a next step (does the model show *hysteresis* when γ is lowered
then raised?). Synthetic illustration only; it says nothing about the disease.

## Dead ends and surprises

- **Surprise:** the off-target-binding objection to "faster female tau" — which I had flagged on
  night 1 as a possible confound — is largely *defeated* by a fluid marker immune to it
  (MTBR-tau243). One of night 1's hedges turned out weaker than the claim it hedged.
- **Kill:** `detection-vs-biological-resilience`, cleanly, on its own founding data. A good night
  by the program's standard.
- **Dead end:** glymphatic sleep-clearance as a settled mechanism — too contested to build on
  tonight.
- **Caution carried forward:** nearly all the pro-causal BBB data is single-group and
  correlational; treat "BBB breakdown causes AD" as unproven.
