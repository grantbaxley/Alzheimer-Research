## Questions asked

- For each failed program, was it the endpoint, the population, the timing, or the target/dose —
  not "the idea"?
- What design or outcome measure could detect genuine improvement (not slower decline) in 6–12
  months?
- As an ecosystem: is "brain as ecosystem" substantive or metaphor? Are there regime shifts /
  alternative stable states? Does the restoration-ecology case for multi-target beat single drugs?
- New questions invented tonight (added to `lenses.yaml`): is the dose-response graded or
  threshold-like, and is the person-level outcome unimodal or bimodal — the signature of a basin
  boundary?

## What I found (with sources)

**Failure anatomy is more diagnostic than "trials keep failing" suggests.** Solanezumab failed at
*both* mild-dementia (EXPEDITION3, p=0.10) and preclinical (A4) stages — and since it binds soluble
Aβ but leaves plaque intact, A4 is the cleanest natural experiment separating "too late" from
"wrong target," and it points to **target**, not timing (PMID 29365294; PMID 37458272). BACE
inhibition (verubecestat) was *worse* at the earlier (prodromal) stage with brain-volume loss — a
mechanism-toxicity failure that directly contradicts the simple "earlier is better" reading of the
amyloid cascade (PMID 30970186; PMID 33253354). The two positive antibodies slow decline ~25–35%
but **nobody improves above baseline**, and donanemab's signal rode on tau-stage enrichment (PMID
36449413; PMID 37459141).

**Two hard constraints for any reversal trial.** First, **amyloid is not a validated cognitive
surrogate**: across 14 RCTs a 0.1-SUVR reduction maps to ≈ +0.03 MMSE points (95% CI −0.06 to 0.1)
— essentially nothing (Ackley 2021, PMID 33632704). Second, and decisive for us: **no cognitive,
digital, speech or fluid measure is validated to detect improvement above a person's own baseline**
over 6–12 months — every instrument is validated for *decline* (PMID 24886908; PMID 41368997).
You cannot yet score a reversal even if one happened. Sex interactions are never pre-powered
(~4× the sample needed), and the sex subgroups point in opposite directions across drugs
(male-favoring lecanemab/aducanumab, female-favoring donanemab) — the fingerprint of underpowered
noise, not biology (PMID 41551731).

**"Brain as ecosystem" is substantive-but-partial.** Genuinely operational in two places:
dynamical-systems modeling of AD (tipping points and early-warning signals formally tested against
ADNI/connectome data — PMID 42899506, PMID 41675964) and multidomain-prevention design (FINGER,
POINTER — the restoration-ecology "re-establish conditions, don't add one species" logic made
real, though effect sizes are small and replications inconsistent, and POINTER's imaging ancillary
was null). Well-supported as descriptive cell biology *if* you drop the two-species caricature —
the A1/A2 astrocyte and M1/M2 microglia binaries are formally deprecated for continua (Escartin
2021, PMID 33589835). Still mostly metaphor where it's loudest: the causal gut-microbiome story is
unproven in humans, and GV-971/sodium oligomannate rests on a single 36-week China trial with
three mutually incompatible proposed mechanisms and heavy developer funding (PMID 33731209; ethics
critique PMID 36625928). A real gap: genuine Lotka-Volterra competition models of brain cell
populations barely exist — the math used is spreading/epidemic, not competition.

## New ideas

Three (detailed in `ideas.yaml`):

1. **regime-shift-trial-signatures** (reverse) — if the brain has alternative stable states,
   recovery is a jump between basins, so trials should hunt the *signatures* — bimodal person-level
   outcomes and a threshold (not graded) dose-response — instead of a group-mean slope that averages
   them away. Both are testable in existing trial data. Built on tonight's model.
2. **microglia-plaque-predator-prey** (understand) — fill the Lotka-Volterra gap with a predator-prey
   model where microglia clear amyloid but *degrade as they feed* (homeostatic → dysfunctional).
   Kill risk: cell states are continua, so a two-species model may re-import the deprecated A1/A2
   caricature.
3. **improvement-endpoint-validation** (detect) — validate a readout that can detect *within-person*
   improvement in 6–12 months against a positive control (recovery from a reversible cause), because
   none exists and no reversal trial can be scored without one. Kill risk: practice effects
   contaminate any "improvement."

## Modeling

`scripts/regime_shift_2026_10_24.py` — an ecological alternative-stable-states toy, built to answer
the question night 4 left open (what feedback structure would make a "point of no return" real,
since the spreading model was *not* bistable). A one-variable homeostasis model with positive
feedback `dh/dt = r·h(1−h) − s + b` under a stress driver `s`. Sweeping stress up then down: the
healthy state collapses at s≈0.36 but only recovers once s falls back to ≈0.10 — clean
**hysteresis**, a gap between the "stop" and "reverse" thresholds. The honest contrast with night 4:
bistability is a property of the *feedback structure you assume*, not wishful thinking — the
spreading model lacked the self-reinforcing term and showed none; this model has it and shows a
wide one. Trial-design reading: *if* the brain behaves like this, halting progression and reversing
it are different thresholds, and the gap is a quantity a trial could aim at. Toy equation, not a
disease model, not evidence the brain is bistable.

## Dead ends and surprises

- **Surprise that cuts against the cascade:** BACE inhibition got *worse* earlier, not better —
  "treat earlier" is not a universal law; it depends on whether the mechanism is also doing
  something useful.
- **The measurement wall:** the most important negative of the night is mundane and decisive — we
  have no validated way to measure improvement. Every reversal ambition runs into this first. Hence
  idea 3.
- **Skeptical catch:** GV-971's three incompatible mechanisms and single-country, developer-funded
  evidence base are a caution against the tidy "fix the microbiome" story; I did not treat it as a
  lead.
- **Honest limit on the model:** the regime-shift hysteresis is assumed into the feedback term; it
  demonstrates a possibility, not a fact about the brain.
