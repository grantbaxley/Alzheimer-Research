## What I found (with sources)

**High-pathology-no-dementia is real and common** — up to ~1/3 of ROS/MAP participants show
better-than-expected cognition for their pathology (10.3389/fnagi.2023.1303912); the Nun Study
(PMID 9127971), SuperAgers (lower tangle density, larger entorhinal layer-II neurons, more von
Economo neurons; 10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1409-14.2015), and the 100-plus centenarians (pathology did not
predict cognition; PLCG2 protective enriched) all say the same thing: **tau/neurodegeneration is
decoupled from amyloid in the resilient**, and amyloid load alone predicts dementia weakly.

**Protective variants do NOT share one mechanism** — they cluster into three partly-overlapping
themes: (a) Reelin/APOE-receptor/HSPG → tau (APOE3-Christchurch, 10.1038/s41591-019-0611-3;
RELN-COLBOS, 10.1038/s41591-023-02318-3 — both lower tau at high amyloid); (b) microglial state
(PLCG2-P522R protective, 10.1038/ng.3916; TREM2 — note R47H is a *risk* loss-of-function, OR ~2.9,
10.1056/NEJMoa1211103, so "protective TREM2" means restoring wild-type); (c) lipid/APOE (APOE2,
10.1038/s41467-019-14279-8; APOE-Jacksonville V236E). Convergence exists only for the two Colombian
variants (the Reelin→Dab1→reduced tau-phosphorylation node).

**Resilience today is mostly a MARKER, not a validated causal lever.** Cognitive-reserve proxies
(education/IQ) are confounded; the Mendelian variants are the only clean causation; multidomain
trials (US POINTER n=2,111; FINGER) move a cognitive composite modestly with no pathology or
dementia endpoint. No intervention has validated a resilience readout as a reversal endpoint.

**Breadcrumbs:** Agora hosts >950 AMP-AD/TREAT-AD nominated targets and the NIA Resilience-AD
program feeds resilience nominations — this **confirms** our `resilience-downstream-effector`
direction. New datapoints (not duplicates): a ROSMAP cell-type-abundance GWAS links TMEM106B
(rs13237518) to neuron abundance *independent of pathology*, and a cognitive-resilience GWAS
(ATP8B1 locus; UKB 13 resilience loci) finds resilience is **not** genetically just inverse-risk. No
AI agent surfaced a specific resilience target tonight (none found, not "none exist").

## Revisited: lithium-microdose-target-trial (new → investigating, with two honest downgrades)

Two things changed since 2026-10-23. (1) The entry's claimed novelty was "the design, not the drug"
— but that active-comparator new-user target-trial emulation now **largely exists**: a 2026 preprint
(medRxiv 10.64898/2026.01.12.26343472) runs lithium vs antiepileptic mood stabilisers across
Medicare/MarketScan/Optum and finds a modest, heterogeneous 5-yr RR ~0.84–0.87 for *progression*
(Optum null; MarketScan 0.57), not peer-reviewed; a 2024 Nordic register trial found no systematic
difference. So the signal weakly survives an active comparator but is not clean, and is about
progression in already-ill patients, not prevention. (2) The 2025 Nature mechanism paper
(10.1038/s41586-025-09335-x) — PFC-specific lithium depletion already at MCI, amyloid sequestration,
de-repressed GSK3β driving tau, orotate (not carbonate) rescuing mice — compared **diagnostic
groups, not resilient-vs-demented at matched pathology**, so "endogenous lithium is a resilience
buffer" is a tempting but *untested* inference. Reframed accordingly, kept at `preclinical`, not
promoted. **Possible convergence worth flagging:** RELN-COLBOS lowers tau via Dab1 and lithium loss
raises tau via GSK3β — plausibly the same kinase node, i.e. a human resilience variant and a
depletable brain metal buffering tau through one effector.

## New ideas (all sourced from outside medicine, per the constraint)

1. **resilience-is-growth-not-topology** (understand, in-silico) — from wildfire firebreaks /
   power-grid islanding / percolation. Tested and the naive form **killed** tonight (below); the
   surviving claim is that resilience against an autocatalytic spreader is a growth-term property.
2. **disease-tolerance-slope-endpoint** (understand, observational) — from the ecology/immunology of
   host defence (10.1126/science.1214935): resilience = *tolerance* (flat cognition-vs-tau slope),
   not resistance (low tau); the decisive readout is the per-person random slope. Kill: no stable
   between-person slope component above measurement error.
3. **response-diversity-not-redundancy** (understand, observational) — from ecology's insurance
   hypothesis (10.1073/pnas.96.4.1463): resilience = redundancy × (1 − cross-circuit susceptibility
   correlation), i.e. *decorrelated* vulnerability, explicitly distinct from our existing
   spare-capacity redundancy metric. Kill: adds nothing beyond the Fiedler metric + education.

## Modeling — a night that kills a borrowed analogy

`scripts/firebreak_percolation_2026_11_05.py`, built on the repo's own `alzres.spreading` (FKPP
reaction-diffusion vs linear NDM, on a synthetic connectome, with effective-resistance edge ranking
and degree-preserving nulls). Two honest results. (1) **FKPP has a sharp invasion threshold in the
GROWTH rate** (α 0.2 → 0% of regions invaded, α 0.3+ → 100%); linear diffusion (NDM) has none and
floods every connected node — so a "resilience threshold" is a property of *autocatalytic dynamics*,
not of the wiring (ties to our open `identify-autocatalysis-rate`). (2) The **firebreak/islanding
analogy FAILS** as a resilience mechanism: down-weighting even the top bottleneck (bridge) edges by
up to 90% did not contain an above-threshold front (each region self-amplifies once seeded); the only
thing that contained it — a full cut — merely isolated the epicenter into a 1-node component (and
contained NDM equally), i.e. trivial disconnection a brain cannot perform. Conclusion: against an
autocatalytic spreader, resilience lives in the **growth term** (the lithium/GSK3β buffer and the
disease-tolerance slope), not in edge topology. Synthetic graph, assumed rates; validates the logic,
not any claim about brains; conditional on tau actually being autocatalytic in humans.
