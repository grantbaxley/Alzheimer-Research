## What I found (with sources)

**Why there — the LC's vulnerability is mostly undruggable, except one node.** The locus coeruleus
holds the earliest AD tau (Braak subcortical pretangles, 10.1097/NEN.0b013e318232a379). Its
vulnerability comes from an extreme unmyelinated axonal arbor + high tonic firing (metabolic/Ca²⁺
load — structural, not druggable), neuromelanin/iron, and one **drug-targetable** node: MAO-A
deaminates noradrenaline to the reactive aldehyde **DOPEGAL**, which activates AEP/legumain to cleave
tau at N368 and seed spread (Kang/Ye, *JCI* 2020, 10.1172/JCI130513; mouse/cell).

**The night's most interesting finding: the field tested the wrong MAO isoform.** The DOPEGAL
mechanism implicates **MAO-A**, but the only completed MAO-inhibitor AD biomarker RCT used **MAO-B**
(rasagiline), which met an FDG-PET endpoint and moved no tau marker (10.1002/trc2.12106). A safe,
approved, cheap **MAO-A** inhibitor (e.g. moclobemide) aimed at DOPEGAL has, as far as can be sourced,
never been trialed in AD. Atomoxetine (NE-reuptake inhibitor) has the cleanest human target-engagement
signal — a 12-month RCT raised CSF NE and lowered CSF total-tau/pTau181 ~5-6%, no cognitive effect
(Levey, *Brain* 2021, 10.1093/brain/awab452). Deferiprone (iron chelation) **failed with harm**
(accelerated decline; Ayton, *JAMA Neurol* 2025, 10.1001/jamaneurol.2024.3733) — a cautionary tale
against naive "remove the toxic metal." Methodology lesson for the lens: healthy-user bias can
manufacture 20-50% spurious "protection" in dementia claims data (the statin target-trial-emulation
collapse) — believe the active-comparator TTE / MR over raw claims.

## Revisited: sex-amyloid-tau-window (stays investigating; sharpened, one mechanism demoted)

Three updates. (1) **Re-anchor the clock as amyloid chronicity** (time-since-amyloid-positivity), not
chronological age or a two-scan tau interval, because the robust human sex effect is on the
**amyloid→tau coupling step, not amyloid accumulation rate** (Coughlan 2025 meta of 6 tau-PET
cohorts, n=1,376, PMID 40029638 — female excess is Aβ+-conditional; Wang *Brain* 2024 locates the sex
term at the coupling step). The best possible news for the idea: the sex term and the leverage point
are the **same node**. (2) **Add LC neuromelanin-MRI** as a cheaper, earlier, tracer-free leading edge
(LC integrity decline precedes MTL tau, Bueichekú/Jacobs *Nat Aging* 2024, PMID 38664576), kept
Aβ-conditional and age-normed (non-monotonic LC curve) and not over-claimed for sex (human LC
dimorphism is "minor"). (3) **Demote the X-linked MAO-A sub-hypothesis** to a clearly-tagged
mechanism-to-test: human MAO-A shows dosage compensation (not a clean double-dose), and the estrogen
link is contradictory (MAO-A binding *peaks in perimenopause*, not with estrogen loss; Rekkas, *JAMA
Psychiatry*). **Breadcrumb:** the LC-seeding genes (MAOA, LGMN/AEP, DBH, ADRA2A) are mechanistically
hot but **genetically cold** for AD (no GWAS nomination) — so this idea is about *timing*, not etiology.

## New ideas

1. **maoa-dopegal-wrong-isoform-repurposing** (slow, preclinical) — trial a reversible MAO-A
   inhibitor (not MAO-B) at the DOPEGAL step. Kill: a ≥12-week MAO-A inhibitor fails to lower CSF
   DOPEGAL / tau-N368 in amyloid+ MCI.
2. **atomoxetine-lc-mri-enrichment** (slow, observational) — repurpose atomoxetine *and* a biomarker,
   enriching by LC neuromelanin-MRI. Kill: LC integrity does not moderate the tau-lowering effect.
3. **perimenopausal-maoa-window** (understand, observational) — test whether the female tau excess
   peaks in a peri-/early-post-menopausal MAO-A window rather than rising monotonically with age.
   Kill: the female-minus-male excess is flat across menopause-proximate age.

(A fourth candidate — combined NET + MAO-A inhibition, to raise functional NE while blocking its toxic
DOPEGAL branch — is noted here as the natural follow-up to idea 2's paradox, not yet a separate entry.)

## Modeling

`scripts/lc_why_there_2026_11_08.py` — a selective-vulnerability ordering toy (dimensionless). Honest
results: (1) the LC seeds first, reproducing the Braak-like order; (2) the LC's **structural** load
alone still ranks it first, so you **cannot stop it being the origin** — the MAO-A/DOPEGAL term is an
accelerant, not the reason; (3) the MAO-A/DOPEGAL branch is the one **druggable** accelerant, and
fully blocking it delays the earliest seed ~60% in the toy — exactly where a safe MAO-A inhibitor (not
the MAO-B one trialed) could act. A clearly-labelled what-if shows the earliest-seed timing is
sensitive to that accelerant (the only honest basis for a sex-aware *timing* clock), with explicit
caveats that human MAO-A sex/estrogen data do not support a clean dose effect. Assumed factors; not
calibrated; not a drug claim.
