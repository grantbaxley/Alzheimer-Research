## What I found (with sources)

**The human "rebuild" column is almost empty.** Adult hippocampal neurogenesis in aged humans is
real but low and falls in AD, and the field is still a fixation-artifact methods war on tens of
autopsy brains (pro: Moreno-Jiménez 2019, 10.1038/s41591-019-0375-9; Terreros-Roncal 2021,
10.1126/science.abl5163; contra: Sorrells 2018, 10.1038/nature25975). No intervention has raised it
in humans. Synaptic density (SV2A-PET) robustly shows **loss** (~41% hippocampal, Chen 2018, PMID
30014145) and **no therapy has ever raised the signal in a human trial** - a clean, unmet reversal
endpoint. Anti-amyloid antibodies slow, they do not reverse.

**Animals can be rebuilt after damage, but replication is the weak point and it's all mouse.** The
strongest on-lens result is brand-new (OSK partial reprogramming of engram neurons restoring memory
in aged and amyloid mice, Neuron 2026 - DOI unverified by the assistant, amyloid-only, no tau,
~2-week follow-up, not independently replicated). Optogenetic engram reactivation recovers "lost"
memories in early AD mice (Roy/Tonegawa 2016, 10.1038/nature17172). The honest synthesis of the
engram literature: **memories held in alive-but-silenced neurons are recoverable; memories whose
neurons are dead are gone.** So "reversal" has a theoretical window - before ensemble loss - and
not after.

**The women question, strongest human-grade legs:** faster longitudinal tau accumulation in women
at matched amyloid (cross-sectional Buckley 2019 was small, ~6%, and the authors said it cannot
explain 2:1; longitudinal cohorts upgrade it, though the assistant could not re-verify two of those
DOIs); APOE4×sex at younger ages (Neu 2017, 10.1001/jamaneurol.2017.2188 - higher risk in ε3/ε4
women ~65-75, converging by ~85); and the menopause bioenergetic transition with a partial
post-menopausal rebound (Mosconi 2021, 10.1038/s41598-021-90084-y). The **surprising** vein is
sex + regeneration: aging reactivates the normally silent second X in the female mouse hippocampus,
and a reactivated X-escapee (Plp1, in oligodendrocytes) improved cognition (Gadek 2025, Sci Adv -
mouse); maternal-X silencing reversal rescued deficits (Abdulai-Saiku 2025, 10.1038/s41586-024-08457-y
- mouse). A latent, age-triggered, myelin-linked regenerative reserve on the second X, with no male
equivalent - entirely mouse, but a rare clean convergence of sex + regeneration + a nameable gene.

**Breadcrumbs:** the sex-specific regenerative angle is a genuine whitespace - no open AI agent
(Biomni-AD, Prima Mente, TxGNN, FutureHouse Robin, Google co-scientist, and a Dec-2025 Gemini-2.5
target-ranking preprint, medRxiv 2025.12.28.25343106) has nominated an X-escape / oligodendrocyte /
sex-specific AD target; they keep converging on the microglial+lipid list (confirms the 2026-11-02
scan). Robin's one regeneration-adjacent repurposing hit was dry-AMD (ripasudil), not AD, and its
headline effect size was later deflated by a third party - treat agent effect sizes skeptically.

## Revisited: vaccine-trained-immunity (new -> investigating)

The idea's **own** falsification test is antigen-independence, and it now has data that leans toward
trained immunity: the recombinant adjuvanted Shingrix out-protects the live-attenuated Zostavax
(Taquet 2024, Nat Med, RMTL 0.83 [0.80-0.87], PMID 39053634) - a replicating live virus should win
if the mechanism were antiviral; the two AS01 vaccines (zoster, RSV) are equivalent
(10.1038/s41541-025-01172-3); and BCG lowers dementia ~20% despite zero shared antigen with VZV
(JAMA Netw Open 2023 HR 0.80; Swedish PLOS ONE 10.1371/journal.pone.0292174, AD-specific NS) - though
only in confounded bladder-cancer cohorts. **Sex-specificity is verified and robust** (Eyting 2025
Nature 10.1038/s41586-025-08800-x, effect markedly greater in women; the Cell 2025 progression study,
men indistinguishable from zero for every outcome) **but it is mechanism-agnostic** - estrogen-enhanced
trained immunity, a stronger female anti-VZV response, and sex-skewed healthy-vaccinee bias all predict
a female signal, so sex does not adjudicate. Recorded as a feature, not as evidence for one mechanism.
No independent (non-originating-group) regression-discontinuity replication yet. Net: the mechanism
half strengthened, the effect-is-real half stayed weak (Kivimäki >100% implied VE in 3/4 natural
experiments, PMID 42754179; Wendeln 2018 training worsened amyloid, PMID 29643512; no RCT). So:
`new -> investigating` - the discriminating test is alive and currently favourable, but the evidence
base is too confounded for "promising."

## New ideas

1. **menopause-timed-plasticity-window** (understand, observational) - time a regenerative
   intervention to menopausal STAGE, not chronological age; the post-menopausal rebound may mark a

2. **x-escape-oligodendrocyte-reversal-triage** (reverse, in-silico) - mine OPEN AD omics for

   enrichment in female non-decliners vs decliners, or equal signal in men.
3. **silent-engram-recoverable-window** (reverse, none) - stage AD by silenced-but-alive vs dead
   engrams to define the only window reversal theory allows; modelled tonight. Kill: at earliest
   detectable AD, synaptic loss and irreversible recall failure are already coincident.

## Modeling

`scripts/silent_engram_window_2026_11_04.py` - a two-compartment toy (silenced-but-alive =
recoverable; dead = not), with a reactivation therapy that restores only the silenced pool, capped
at the surviving-neuron ceiling. Honest results: (1) recoverable gain is **front-loaded** - it peaks
early and collapses late as the dead pool overtakes the silenced pool; (2) this reproduces the
recurring "treat early" wall from a **new** mechanism (alive-vs-dead, not reversible-vs-banked). I
dropped an earlier "halving efficiency halves benefit" line because recovered gain is linear in
efficiency, so that is arithmetic, not a finding; the only real point kept is that reactivation
efficiency is a multiplier acting on top of timing, so a collapsed plasticity context and a late
start compound. Structural toy, assumed rates; "reactivation" has never raised human synaptic density.
